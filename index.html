    Customer review:

    Keith Pegram
    (Manydown Estate) based in Basingstoke, Hampshire.

    At Manydown Estate, Keith uses the Calibrator to process green waste and manure for composting, and to prepare material for the estate’s anaerobic digestion system. Getting a consistent particle size is what makes both processes work well, and it is where he has seen the biggest difference.

    Keith says the Calibrator produces a very uniform size, at a speed that is difficult to achieve with other machines. Because the material breaks down more evenly, he believes it has the potential to reduce compost-making time by up to six months, while giving a more reliable feedstock for AD.
